# trell0

minimalist productivity tool. try it out at https://trell0.vercel.app/.

## what is a trello board?

also known as a Kanban board, a trello board is a way to organize anything. think of it as a to-do list but better. you have three columns: to-do, in progress, and done. this can be used to manage your technical projects, your school homework, or any endeavors you may have!

every trello board on the market is sluggish and built for corporate teams. i wanted to build something for the new generation.
